# Star Trek: The Promethean Prophecy

A walkthrough from the Enterprise to the end, as a list of commands to type, place by place. The game understands full sentences, so commands are given as typed; directions are `N`, `S`, `E`, `W`, `NE`, `NW`, `SE`, `SW`, `U` (up) and `D` (down), and aboard the ship `Fore` and `Aft`.

> [!NOTE]
> Played through in Stardate from the bridge to the winning ending ("Congratulations, Captain!"). It works as written, with a few clarifications added below where the original was too brief.

## Before you start

- **The communicator** rings three times during the game. When it does, `turn on communicator`, listen to the message, then `turn off communicator`. At the third call, a Promethean Defender snatches it and crushes it under his boot; that's meant to happen, and you need it broken.
- **Sandstorms:** always flee to the nearest gate, and type `In` to go through it. The inhabitants let you in, then force you out when the storm has passed. It's a good idea to draw a map, so you know where the nearest gate is. A storm can kill the whole landing party, and they can come while you're trading outside a gate.
- **Trading** gives different things at different times: if a trade doesn't give you what the walkthrough says, keep trading back and forth until it does. Check what you're carrying with `Inventory`.

## Aboard the Enterprise

1. `Get holo`, `Examine holo`, `Look at Berryman`, `Berryman, analyze planet`, `Wait`.
2. `Examine viewscreen`, `Berryman, analyze attacker`, `Kirk to Spock`.
3. `Sulu, arm torpedoes`, `Wait`, `Wait`, `Examine bird of prey`.
4. `Yes` (in reply to Uhura). Then `Wait` until Spock reports "an indistinct data image mirroring the movements of our adversary": until he has, firing at it gets "You can't see any image here", and a wasted shot puts the rest of this section out of step.
5. `Fire torpedo at image` four times, until the Bird of Prey is "scorched and inert".
6. `Yes` (when Uhura offers the Romulans' transmission), `Ask Spock about damage`.
7. `Aft`, `D`, `D`, `Fore`, `Fore`.
8. `Get phaser and communicator`, `Examine transporter`, `Get on transporter`, `Energize`.

## Sandy Clearing

1. `Examine obelisk`, `Examine pictogram`, `S`, `Examine scuttler`, `Wait`, `Get gold sphere`.
2. `N`, `N`, `N`, `Get tablet`, `Examine tablet`.
3. `N`, `E`, `E`, `NE`.
4. `Give holo to Weelomin`: he gives you a yellow gem.
5. `Give gold sphere to Weelomin`, and keep trading things back to him until you get the forked thing (it may come on the first trade).
6. `SW`, `W`, `W`, `W`, `W`, `NW`, `NW`, `N`, `NE`, `SE`, `SE`.

## Abandoned Enclave

1. `W`, `Get strand`, `Examine table`.
2. `Set phaser to narrow beam`, `Fire phaser at table`.
3. `Drop all but tablet`, `Get table`, `E`, `Put table on quicksand`.
4. `S`, `Wait`, `Show tablet to voice`, `Break tablet`.
5. `U`, `Wait`, `Wait`, `Wait`: you get a red gem.
6. `D`, `N`, `W`, `Get all`, `E`, `NW`, `D`, `E`, `N`.

## Northern Promontory

1. `Put forked thing in crevice`, `Put strand on forked thing`.
2. `D`, `Get tarpaulin`, `U`, `Get forked thing and strand`.
3. `E`, `S`, `Get on bowl`.
4. Now `Wait`, answering the communicator when it whistles, until a Defender breaks it. It's the third call, about 25 turns in: "Prophecies--these are lies!" Spock picks up the pieces: "That was costly."
5. Only then, `Put tarpaulin on bowl`: the bowl carries you into the Enclave of the Sustainers.

## Enclave of Sustainers

1. `Show communicator to Jharpe`: he repairs it and gives you a green gem. (If it isn't broken yet, he "senses no need for repair", and there's no gem.)
2. `E`, `Open box`, `Get box`, `W`, `Show box to Jharpe`.
3. `NE`, `E`, `S`, `W`, `S`.
4. `Give box to Weelomin`: you get an embroidered robe.
5. `Give communicator to Weelomin`, and keep trading until you get the gold sphere.
6. `Show yellow gem to Defender`.
7. `Give forked thing to Trader`: you get a brush.
8. `Give red gem to Trader`, and keep trading until you get the small sphere.
9. `E`, `SW`, `W`, `W`.

## Blue Gate

1. `S`, then `Wait` until the sandstorm hits, then `N`, `N`.
2. After the sandstorm: `W`, `W`, `NW`, `NW`, `N`.
3. `Ask fool about gold sphere`, and wait until he tells you about the edge of the desert.
4. `S`, `SE`, `SE`, `E`, `E`. (If that has already happened, start here instead.)
5. `S`, `S`, `S`, `S`.

## Edge of Desert

1. `Set phaser to wide beam`, `Get polyp`. The polyp is one of the Fool's illusions, and reaching for it is what brings the burrowers.
2. `Fire phaser at burrowers` again and again. Each sphere turns up in the sand as you fight ("a ribbed sphere is dislodged from the crusty sand", "you notice another sphere in the sand. This one is violet"): pick each one up at once, with `Get ribbed sphere` and `Get violet sphere`.
3. As soon as you have both, `Fire phaser at burrowers` once more, then `NW`. Without that last shot, a burrower is waiting for you when you come out of the cave. Don't stay longer than that, though: the burrowers close in with every move.

## Cave

1. `Spock, scan cave`, if you like, but it only gives advice, and every move here lets the burrowers come closer. It's safest to skip it.
2. `Set phaser to overload`, `Drop phaser`, `SE`.
3. `NW` straight away, then `Get rod`, `Examine rod`, `Spock, scan rod`.
4. `E`, `N`, `N`, `N`, `N`, `E`, `E`, `NE`.

## Outside Yellow Gate

1. `Give brush to Weelomin`: you get a wrinkled robe.
2. `Give violet sphere to Weelomin`: you get a statue. `Examine statue`.
3. `Show yellow gem to defender`.
4. `Give knotted strand to trader`: you get a ragged robe.
5. `Give statue to trader`: you get a new robe.
6. `E`, `Wear embroidered robe`, `Give wrinkled robe to Spock`, `Give new robe to Dimas`, `Give ragged robe to McCoy`.
7. `SW`, `W`, `W`, `Join procession`. If the procession isn't there, wait until it is.

## Cloister of the Chanters

1. `Watch conclave`, `Play rod`: you get a blue gem.
2. `S`, `E`, `E`, `NE`.
3. Trade gems with Weelomin until you have three spheres, one of them the golden sphere. You need the golden, ribbed, small and violet spheres in the pod; the violet one went for the statue, and a gem (the blue, say) buys it back.
4. `SW`, `W`, `W`, `W`, `W`, `NW`, `E`, `Enter pod`.

## In the Pod

1. `Gaidren, tell me about abyss`.
2. `Put golden sphere in light`. When you give the spheres, it asks which delighter each is for:
   - `Give ribbed sphere to delighter`: mumbling.
   - `Give small sphere to delighter`: smiling.
   - `Give violet sphere to delighter`: spinning. You get a violet gem.
3. Take the spheres back (the golden sphere and the rest) until you have all of them.
4. `Out`, `W`, `SE`, `E`, `E`, `E`, `E`, `NE`.

## Outside Yellow Gate, again

1. A sandstorm may be blowing as you arrive (McCoy's joke about "Oklahoma rain" is the first sign), and then Weelomin and the Defender are gone. Type `In` at once to shelter inside the gate; when the storm has passed you're put back outside, and Weelomin returns.
2. Trade the spheres with Weelomin until you have all the gems back except the red gem: red, blue, green, violet and yellow go on the rod, so check with `Inventory` that you have the other four.
3. `Show yellow gem to defender`. If he isn't there, wait until he returns. Showing it takes you through the gate into the Bazaar of the Traders, which is also shelter if a sandstorm comes.
4. Trade with the trader there until you get the red gem. It can take many trades: the trader's goods come round in a cycle (the forked thing, the knotted strand, the statue, the small sphere, the small disk, and so on), and in our play the red gem came on the eleventh.
5. `E` (out of the bazaar), `SW`, `W`, `W`, `Join procession`. Make sure everyone is wearing their robes.

## Cloister of the Chanters, again

1. `Put red gem on rod`, `Put blue gem on rod`, `Put green gem on rod`, `Put violet gem on rod`, `Put yellow gem on rod`.
2. `Play rod`, `Spin rod`, `Wait`. The Afflictor appears.
3. `Wait`, `Give rod to afflictor`.

The mound turns out to be a living spacecraft, the Julga's Seed of Far Places, roused from a long hibernation; the krill the Afflictor made them hoard feeds the Enterprise too, and the Julga escort you back to your ship. "Congratulations, Captain! ... The Federation has come to expect no less of you, but--you've done it again, Jim."
