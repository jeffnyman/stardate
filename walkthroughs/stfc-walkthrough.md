# Star Trek: First Contact

A walkthrough from the bridge of the Enterprise to the end, as a list of orders and moves, place by place.

> [!NOTE]
> Not yet checked in Stardate: this is the original walkthrough, reformatted, with its spelling corrected. The *Kobayashi Alternative* and *Promethean Prophecy* walkthroughs both needed corrections once they were played, so this one may too.

## How the game is played

You are Captain James T. Kirk. You can do things yourself, through the menus, or give orders to the crew.

- **The menus:** F1 opens the menu. From there, F2 is Inventory, F3 Give, F4 Adjust, F5 Touch, F6 Orders, F7 Use, F8 Examine, F9 Look and F10 Utilities. Key sequences below, such as `F4 F1`, are the menu keys pressed in order.
- **Orders** go to the person you're speaking to. To change who that is, type their name (`Sulu`); for someone in another room, use the intercom (`Kirk to Scotty`). F6 lists almost all the orders there are. Below, `Sulu`, `Orbit Hearth` means: type `Sulu`, then type `Orbit Hearth`.
- **Moving:** use the cursor keys. The third column of the green window shows where you can go. In the turbolift, name a deck or a place (`Deck 7`, `Transporter room`).

In the walkthrough, *what people say* is in italics, and moves and other actions are in plain text.

## The bridge: Admiral Tecar

You start on the bridge of the USS Enterprise, NCC-1701, in the Sirius system. *Uhura has orders from Starfleet: orbit Hearth, in the Sirius system, and await a mission briefing.*

1. `Sulu`, `Orbit Hearth`.
2. *Uhura: the Enterprise is to stand by to receive Fleet Admiral Tecar.* *Renner: Admiral Tecar has beamed aboard safely, and asks you to escort him to the bridge.*
3. Go to the turbolift (cursor down). `Transporter room`.
4. Enter deck 7 (cursor up). Walk along the corridors (cursor left or right) until you meet Lieutenant Gomez and Admiral Tecar.
5. Speaking to Tecar: `Follow me`.
6. Go to the turbolift (cursor left or right) and enter it. `Bridge`, then enter the bridge.
7. *Tecar: "Jim, may I address the crew?"* `Yes`.
8. *Tecar: the Enterprise must orbit and hail Starbase Lambda, in the Centauri system. There, the Federation's chosen representatives to the coronation of Prince Clavis III of Gothica will beam aboard.*
9. Tecar takes you aside and gives you a sealed envelope, with orders for a top-secret mission. `Examine secret orders`: now you know what the real mission is.

## Starbase Lambda: the passengers

1. `Sulu`, `Set course to Centauri`, `Warp 5`.
2. When you arrive: `Sulu`, `Orbit Lambda`.
3. `Uhura`, `Open hailing freq to Lambda`.
4. *Uhura: a message from Starbase Lambda; the diplomats are ready to beam up.* `Yes`.
5. *Spock suggests meeting the guests in the Visitor's Lounge.* Enter the turbolift, `Visitor's lounge`, and go to the lounge.
6. Spock lets out a wolf whistle as Nurse Chapel walks by. Strange, isn't it? Remember it for later.
7. *Chambers: "Permission to report, sir?"* `Yes`.
8. After the introductions, Phlogis am-Greel tells you about the meeting in the briefing room, and suggests going to Gothica at warp 1.

## Sick bay and engineering

1. *Dr. Hodges wants you to escort him to sick bay, to meet Dr. McCoy.* `Yes`.
2. Go to the turbolift, `Sick bay`, and go to sick bay. Nurse Chapel tells you about Dr. McCoy's strange behavior. First Spock, then McCoy: keep it in mind.
3. Enter sick bay and deliver Dr. Hodges.
4. Try to go to the bridge. Scotty interrupts: *"Cap'n, could I be havin' a word with ya in engineering? Aye, I'd like to have Mr. Spock's keen mind on this too, as soon as you can."*
5. Go to engineering, on deck 7. `Kirk to Spock`, `Come here`.
6. Surprise: Denjik, of the planet Seltik, wants to join the crew. A shapeshifter might come in handy. `Yes`.

## The briefing room: the impostors

1. *Johanson: "Captain, please meet me in the briefing room. It is urgent."*
2. Go to the turbolift, `Briefing room`, go to the briefing room, and enter it.
3. *Johanson: "Permission to report, Captain?"* `Yes`.
4. `Examine list`.
5. `Spock`, `Give list`, `Analyze list`. When Spock comes back: `Results`. Another impostor.
6. Go to Populski's stateroom, on deck 5, and enter it. You find Populski's body: this shapeshifter isn't as friendly as the first.
7. `Kirk to Bones`, `Come here`.
8. *Uhura: a message from Starfleet on the priority channel, for your eyes only.* Go to the bridge.
9. `Uhura`, `Give message`, `Examine message`. It confirms what you had worked out already.
10. *Spock: only two suspects are left. The two Formid women have no alibis.*

## On the way to Gothica: the Formid

1. `Sulu`, `Set course to Gothica`, `Warp 1`.
2. Learn about Formid culture from the computer: `Computer data`, then `Formid`, `D'nis`, `M'ree`, `Thembo root` and `Formid cuisine`.
3. Go to the Officers' dining room. Enter the Formid stateroom, then the chamber. `Get thembo root`.
4. Leave the stateroom and enter the dining room.
5. *Swarz: "Any special food requests?"* `Yes`, `Give thembo root`.
6. *Swarz: "Whose food should I put it in, sir?"* `M'ree`.
7. *Spock: "Should I call security?"* `Yes`.
8. *Spock: "We can proceed to Gothica in safety now, Captain."*

## Gothica: the coronation

1. Go to the bridge. `Sulu`, `Warp 8`.
2. When you arrive in the Ashadha system: `Orbit Gothica`.
3. Go to the transporter room. `Renner`, `Prepare landing party`, `Beam us down`.
4. Go to the royal chapel (right, up, up), and watch the coronation and what follows.
5. *Whitlea: "Will you help us to recover the sceptre?"* `Yes`.
6. Go to the royal chapel (left), then to the trash basket (right, down, down).
7. `Get tube of glue`, `Get trash basket`, `Examine trash basket`. Spock suggests the assassin was in disguise.
8. `Examine nils`, `Examine smudge`. But where is the sceptre?

## The pursuit

1. *Sulu: an alien craft has been spotted fleeing toward Klingon space, and Prince Clavis asks the Enterprise to help pursue it. Shall we pursue?*
2. **Save the game here.** Then `Yes`.
3. In the battle that follows, you have to be very quick, so use the function keys. F6 is Orders, and F2 in it is Attack Orders:
   - `F6 F2 F6`: raise screens.
   - `F6 F2 F5`: lock torpedoes. `F6 F2 F2`: fire torpedoes.
   - `F6 F2 F5`: lock torpedoes again. Wait a few seconds for the torpedoes to reload, then `F6 F2 F2`: fire torpedoes.
4. You arrive in the Tahl system.

## Avon

1. `Kirk to Sulu`, `Orbit Avon`.
2. `Renner`, `Prepare landing party`, `Beam us down`.
3. **Save the game here.**
4. Cursor down. Now you know what's happening here, and why the planet is called Avon. Be quick again: Brutus kills you after a random number of moves.
5. Cursor up until you're on the theater stage. Enter the right wings (cursor right).
6. `Adjust phaser on` (`F4 F1`).
7. `Use phaser on curtain rope` (`F7 F2`).
8. You learn the story of the planet Avon, and are brought to a spaceship.
9. Enter the airlock (cursor right), then the control room (cursor right). An unknown satellite is shown orbiting the planet. *Sulu: "... you should beam up ..."*
10. `Adjust communicator on` (`F4 F2`). `Kirk to Renner`, `Beam us up`.

## The satellite: the endgame

1. `Kirk to Sulu`, `Orbit satellite`.
2. `Renner`, `Prepare landing party`, `Beam us down`. The endgame begins.
3. Cursor down.
4. **Test 1:** `Examine rug`. `Spock`, `Tell me about rug`.
5. **Test 2:** `Touch sculptures` (`F5 F5`). *Voice: "What do the sculptures represent?"* `The alphabet`.
6. **Test 3:**
   1. `Touch nullifier` (`F5 F5`). *Voice: "Answer this riddle: what expands to conform to its invisible contents?"* `Balloon`, then `Take balloon`.
   2. `Touch nullifier` (`F5 F5`). *Voice: "Now answer this riddle: what is the most excited state of nature?"* `Gas`, then `Take canister`.
   3. `Use canister on balloon` (`F7 F7 F5`).
   4. `Use balloon on nullifier` (`F7 F6 F10`).
7. **Test 4:** left, left, down, down.
8. Up. The game is solved: *"Congratulations, you've made First Contact!"*
