# Star Trek: First Contact

A walkthrough from the bridge of the Enterprise to the end, as a list of orders and moves, place by place.

> [!NOTE]
> Played through in Stardate from the bridge to the ending, contact with the Zontarites. The original walkthrough mostly holds, but it needed corrections in places: Uhura's message, the thembo root, the trash basket, the battle (whose orders as written always lose), the shields at Avon, and the last two tests. They're made below.

## How the game is played

You are Captain James T. Kirk. You can do things yourself, through the menus, or give orders to the crew.

- **The menus:** F1 opens the menu. From there, F2 is Inventory, F3 Give, F4 Adjust, F5 Touch, F6 Orders, F7 Use, F8 Examine, F9 Look and F10 Utilities; each also works on its own, without F1. What Kirk does himself, he does through these menus: below, `Examine secret orders` means F8 Examine, then the secret orders. Typing it instead only says it aloud, and gets *"No one hears you"*, or a puzzled crewman.
- **Menu items** are listed by name, with a function key beside each, and a long list goes on over pages (`F10 ...MORE`). The keys change with what's at hand, so pick items by name; key sequences given below, such as `F4 F1`, are only what they were in one play.
- **Taking things:** open the Inventory (F2). The left column, *In the area*, is what's here; press an item's key to pick it up, and Esc to close the box.
- **Orders** go to the person you're speaking to. To change who that is, type their name (`Sulu`); for someone in another room, use the intercom (`Kirk to Scotty`). F6 lists almost all the orders there are. Below, `Sulu`, `Orbit Hearth` means: type `Sulu`, then type `Orbit Hearth`.
- **Moving:** use the cursor keys. The third column of the green window shows where you can go. In the turbolift, name a deck or a place (`Deck 7`, `Transporter room`); it doesn't know every place (not the dining room, say), but it always knows the decks.
- **Waiting:** there's no `Wait` order (*"You must use accepted Starfleet protocol"*). Time passes by itself, so to wait, just wait. A "Press any key to continue" box stops the clock until a key is pressed.
- **The computer:** `Computer data` opens it, each topic you type is looked up, and Esc closes it.

In the walkthrough, *what people say* is in italics, and moves and other actions are in plain text.

## The bridge: Admiral Tecar

You start on the bridge of the USS Enterprise, NCC-1701, in the Sirius system. *Uhura has orders from Starfleet: orbit Hearth, in the Sirius system, and await a mission briefing.*

1. `Sulu`, `Orbit Hearth`.
2. *Uhura: the Enterprise is to stand by to receive Fleet Admiral Tecar.* *Renner: Admiral Tecar has beamed aboard safely, and asks you to escort him to the bridge.* Do: if you leave him, he finds his way to the bridge alone, put out, and you don't get the secret orders.
3. Go to the turbolift (cursor down). `Transporter room`.
4. Enter deck 7 (cursor up). Walk along the corridors (cursor left or right) until you meet Lieutenant Gomez and Admiral Tecar (in our play, three steps left).
5. `Tecar`, `Follow me`.
6. Go to the turbolift (cursor left or right) and enter it. `Bridge`, then enter the bridge.
7. *Tecar: "Jim, may I address the crew?"* `Yes`.
8. *Tecar: the Enterprise must orbit and hail Starbase Lambda, in the Centauri system. There, the Federation's chosen representatives to the coronation of Prince Clavis III of Gothica will beam aboard.*
9. Tecar takes you aside and gives you a sealed envelope, with orders for a top-secret mission. `Examine secret orders`: now you know what the real mission is.

## Starbase Lambda: the passengers

1. `Sulu`, `Set course to Centauri`, `Warp 5`.
2. When you arrive: `Sulu`, `Orbit Lambda`.
3. `Uhura`, `Open hailing freq to Lambda`.
4. *Uhura: a message from Starbase Lambda; the diplomats are ready to beam up.* `Yes`.
5. *Spock suggests meeting the guests in the Visitor's Lounge.* Enter the turbolift, `Visitor's lounge`, leave it on deck 5 (cursor up), and go one step left: the lounge is up.
6. Spock lets out a wolf whistle as Nurse Chapel walks by. Strange, isn't it? Remember it for later.
7. *Chambers: "Permission to report, sir?"* `Yes`.
8. After the introductions, Phlogis am-Greel tells you about the meeting in the briefing room, and suggests going to Gothica at warp 1.

## Sick bay and engineering

1. *Dr. Hodges wants you to escort him to sick bay, to meet Dr. McCoy.* `Yes`.
2. Go to the turbolift, `Sick bay`, and go to sick bay: on deck 7, one step left, its door is down. Nurse Chapel may stop you on the way, worried about Dr. McCoy's strange behavior. First Spock, then McCoy: keep it in mind.
3. Enter sick bay and deliver Dr. Hodges: McCoy takes him off to his office.
4. Try to go to the bridge. Scotty interrupts: *"Cap'n, could I be havin' a word with ya in engineering? Aye, I'd like to have Mr. Spock's keen mind on this too, as soon as you can."*
5. Go to engineering, on deck 7 (`Engineering` in the turbolift, then one step right: its door is down). `Kirk to Spock`, `Come here`.
6. Surprise: Denjik, of the planet Seltik, wants to join the crew. A shapeshifter might come in handy. `Yes`.

## The briefing room: the impostors

1. *Johanson: "Captain, please meet me in the briefing room. It is urgent."*
2. Go to the turbolift, `Briefing room`, then along deck 5 to the briefing room (in our play, one step right; its door is up), and enter it.
3. *Johanson: "Permission to report, Captain?"* `Yes`.
4. `Examine list`.
5. `Spock`, `Give list`, `Analyze list`. When Spock comes back: `Results`. Another impostor.
6. Go to Populski's stateroom, on deck 5, and enter it: from the briefing room's door, four steps left, then down. (Deck 5's corridor is a ring of eight steps, with eight staterooms; the others belong to Chambers, Dr. Hodges, Minister Stewart, Kurt van der Wyse, Phlogis am-Greel, Kavalas ra-Blar and Xlitf.) You find Populski's body: this shapeshifter isn't as friendly as the first.
7. `Kirk to Bones`, `Come here`.
8. *Uhura: a message from Starfleet on the priority channel, for your eyes only.* Go to the bridge.
9. `Uhura`, `Give me the message` (not `Give message`: she says *"Yes, Captain"* and keeps it), then `Examine message`. It confirms what you had worked out already.
10. *Spock, a little later: only two suspects are left. The two Formid women have no alibis.*

## On the way to Gothica: the Formid

1. `Sulu`, `Set course to Gothica`, `Warp 1`.
2. Learn about Formid culture from the computer: `Computer data`, then `Formid`, `D'nis`, `M'ree`, `Thembo root` and `Formid cuisine`. Esc closes the computer.
3. *Tanzer: a cocktail party for the delegates, in the Officers' Dining Room at 0:37.* Go to deck 5 (`Deck 5` in the turbolift) and along to the dining room: in our play, four steps left. Opposite it, through the door down, is the Formid suite; go in, and down again into the chamber.
4. Take the thembo root, from the Inventory's *In the area*.
5. Up, up and up: back through the suite and the corridor into the dining room. Wait there for the party.
6. *Swarz: "Any special food requests?"* `Yes`, `Give thembo root`.
7. *Swarz: "Whose food should I put it in, sir?"* `M'ree`.
8. *Spock: "Should I call security?"* `Yes`. M'ree turns out to be Lek-Varr, a Klingon agent.
9. *Spock, after Gomez's report: "We can proceed to Gothica in safety now, Captain."*

## Gothica: the coronation

1. Go to the bridge (out of the dining room, down, then right to the turbolift). `Sulu`, `Warp 8`.
2. When you arrive in the Ashadha system: `Orbit Gothica`.
3. Go to the transporter room (`Transporter room` in the turbolift, then three steps right on deck 7: its door is down). `Renner`, `Prepare landing party`, `Beam us down`.
4. Go to the royal chapel (right, up, up), and watch the coronation and what follows.
5. *Whitlea: "Will you help us to recover the sceptre?"* `Yes`.
6. Go to the royal chapel (left), then to the trash basket (right, down: it's one step down, not two).
7. Take the tube of glue and the trash basket, from the Inventory's *In the area*. `Examine trash basket`: hair and glue. Spock suggests the assassin was in disguise.
8. Wait for Lord Demmon, Minister Whitlea and the guards. Then `Examine Nils` (he's on the Examine menu's second page): a smudge above his lip. Whitlea has him arrested. But where is the sceptre?

## The pursuit

1. *Sulu: an alien craft has been spotted fleeing toward Klingon space, and Prince Clavis asks the Enterprise to help pursue it. Shall we pursue?*
2. **Save the game here.** Then `Yes`.
3. You're beamed up to the transporter room, and the Enterprise goes to maximum warp. Step out into the corridor; soon after, in Klingon space, *Sulu: "Klingon starship, Captain, closing fast."*
4. Give your orders through F6 Orders, F2 Attack Orders:
   - `F6 F2 F6`: raise screens.
   - `F6 F2 F9`: divert power. *Sulu: "Divert to which ship's system, sir?"* `Shields`.

   The original walkthrough's orders (screens, then two salvos of torpedoes, locked and fired) don't work: in our play the first salvo always gave only a glancing blow, the second never came in time, and the Enterprise was destroyed ("We're sitting ducks, Captain!"), whatever the timing. With power diverted to the shields, the Klingon is gone.
5. You arrive in the Tahl system.

## Avon

1. `Kirk to Sulu`, `Orbit Avon`.
2. Lower the shields, or Renner can't work the transporter: `Kirk to Sulu`, then `F6 F2 F7` (lower screens). The order goes to whoever you're speaking to, so it must be Sulu.
3. Into the transporter room (down from the corridor). `Renner`, `Prepare landing party`, `Beam us down`. You meet Hamlet, in the Town Square.
4. **Save the game here.**
5. Cursor down. Now you know what's happening here, and why the planet is called Avon. Be quick again: Brutus kills you after a random number of moves.
6. Cursor up until you're on the theater stage (four steps up, to the theatre, and one more onto the stage). Enter the right wings (cursor right), where an old frayed rope holds the curtain.
7. `Adjust phaser on` (F4 Adjust, then the phaser).
8. `Use phaser on curtain rope` (F7 Use, the phaser, then the curtain rope).
9. You learn the story of the planet Avon, and are brought to a spaceship.
10. Enter the airlock (cursor right), then the control room (cursor right). An unknown satellite is shown orbiting the planet. *Sulu: "... you should beam up ..."*
11. `Adjust communicator on` (F4 Adjust, then the communicator). `Kirk to Renner`, `Beam us up`.

## The satellite: the endgame

1. `Kirk to Sulu`, `Orbit satellite`.
2. `Renner`, `Prepare landing party`, `Beam us down`. The endgame begins: a Zontarite voice sets you five tests.
3. Cursor down.
4. **Test 1:** `Examine rug`. `Spock`, `Tell me about rug`.
5. **Test 2:** `Touch sculptures` (`F5 F5`). *Voice: "What do the sculptures represent?"* `The alphabet`.
6. **Test 3:**
   1. `Touch nullifier` (F5 Touch, then the nullifier: it may take a moment to appear on the menu). *Voice: "Answer this riddle: what expands to conform to its invisible contents?"* `Balloon`, then `Take balloon`.
   2. `Touch nullifier`. *Voice: "Now answer this riddle: what is the most excited state of nature?"* `Gas`, then `Take canister`.
   3. `Use canister on balloon`.
   4. `Use balloon on nullifier` (the nullifier is on the second page of what the balloon can be used on).
7. **Test 4:** *Voice: "The door is plainly visible. All you must do is walk to the next room..."* The room shifts as you walk. Left, left, down, down puts the door in front of you; then down again, through it, into the white room. (The original walkthrough has up here, which goes nowhere.)
8. **Test 5**, which the original walkthrough leaves out: *Voice: "Whoever passes through the door first, dies... Choose, Captain."* Go through the door yourself (up). The Zontarites put Nils, the failed assassin, in Kirk's place, welcome the Enterprise, and contact is made. The game ends there, with the publisher's credit.
