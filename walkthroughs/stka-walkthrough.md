# Star Trek: The Kobayashi Alternative

A walkthrough for the IBM PC release (version 2.0, from 1987), the one Stardate plays. It follows the shortest route found through the game, played headless in Stardate from a new game and checked step by step, with every order given exactly as typed. It differs a good deal from the walkthroughs written for the Apple II version: Orna can't be skipped, Achir has a time bomb, and the fight at Rird and the ending at the protostar work differently.

> [!NOTE]
> The good ending hasn't been reached yet. The route gets you to the final scene at the protostar, but beaming the antimatter down there, as described below, ends with the star going nova. What the game checks to choose the good ending is still unknown; see [The protostar](#the-protostar).

## Playing

### Keys

| Key | Function |
|---|---|
| F1 | Help |
| F2 | Inventory |
| F3 | Give |
| F4 | Take |
| F5 | Use |
| F6 | Shoot |
| F7 | Examine |
| F8 | Look |
| F9 | Drop |
| F10 | Utilities (save, restore, and so on) |

The arrow keys move you: aboard the Enterprise, Up and Down go in and out of rooms and Left and Right walk along a corridor; on a planet's surface they walk north, south, west and east. Anything typed is said aloud, to whoever you name first (`Spock, scan the planet`) or to the last person you named.

### Things worth knowing

- **Starfleet words.** The game understands only the phrases in its instructions (`games/stka/STDOC.TXT`) and a few more, such as `who are you`, `can you help us` and `have you seen the Klingons`. Anything else gets "Commands must include Starfleet words".
- **Replies.** A reply stays on the screen until you press a key, and that key is used up closing it. Wait for the game to finish drawing before you type, or the start of your order is lost.
- **Don't type a full line.** An order long enough to fill the line ends the game.
- **Two things in your hands.** If you're holding three things when you leave a room, the last one in the inventory's list disappears. Keep two in your hands and the rest in your pack: in the inventory (F2), the key beside an item moves it between the two.
- **Clothes.** Uniform, spacesuit, coldgear and heatgear are changed in the transporter room: open the inventory (F2) and press F1 until the one you want is shown.
- **The communicator** is switched on and off with Use (F5). It doesn't work underground, or in some caves.
- **The crew.** `Spock, follow me` and the like bring crew along; they stop following when you beam back aboard, so ask again. Someone elsewhere on the ship can be reached with `Kirk to Scotty` and called with `come to me`.
- **The transporter** works only from the transporter room, only in orbit, and only with the screens lowered.
- **Save often** (F10). The fight at Rird and the end at the protostar both depend on timing.

## Orna

The game opens with the Enterprise stranded in the iota Trianguli system after a battle, its engines destroyed. Spock: "We will need assistance from the Ornae to repair them." Scotty can't fix them, and the ship can't leave until the Ornae do.

1. On the bridge, `Spock, follow me`. Leave by the turbolift (Down), say `transporter`, and walk the deck 7 corridor to Sickbay for `McCoy, follow me`, then on to the transporter room. Take the communicator and the tricorder there (F4), and put the tricorder in your pack.
2. Back on the bridge: `Chekov, set a course for Orna`, `orbit the second planet`, then `lower the screens`. An ornacraft appears; scanning it shows signals coming from inside the eastern mountain range.
3. In the transporter room: `Renner, set coordinates at 7650.8150`, `beam us down`. You're in a tunnel running east and west.
4. Walk east about six steps into the great cavern of the Ornae, master mechanics, at about 7650.8300.
5. `Ornae, can you help us`. A party of Ornae beam aboard with their tools and fix the engines in ten minutes: "If anything *ever* stops working again, we'll fix it...free."
6. The communicator doesn't work in the caves. Walk west out of the tunnel to the valley (about 7650.7575), switch the communicator on with F5, then `Kirk to Renner`, `beam us up`.

## The tapes

Before leaving Orna, fetch the videotapes from the recreation deck for Bhrf, still in uniform: from the turbolift say `deck 8`, walk to the Recdeck, take the tapes (F4) and put them in your pack. Then go back to the transporter room, change into the spacesuit for Achir, and `Spock, follow me`.

## Achir

> [!WARNING]
> The Klingons have left a time bomb on Achir. It goes off 500 game seconds (about 8 1/3 minutes) after the Enterprise goes into orbit there, and the game ends. Do everything you can before ordering the orbit.

The bomb only appears once you've talked with the five tribes of Achir, in order. Any question will do; `who are you` is the quickest.

1. From the bridge: `Chekov, set a course for Achir`, `warp factor 6`. On arrival in the R Octantis system, don't orbit yet.
2. Walk to the transporter room first, then order the orbit by intercom: `Kirk to Chekov`, `orbit the first planet`. Renner sets a landing site in the master dome: `Renner, beam us down`.
3. **The Melucht**, the architect, at 7600.11400: `Melucht, who are you`. (Asked `have you seen the Klingons`, it tells you about the bomb.)
4. **The Harapha**, at 6750.11400, 12 steps south: `Harapha, who are you`.
5. **The Asuiar**, at 6700.15100: `Kirk to Renner`, `beam us up`, `Renner, set coordinates at 6700.15100`, `beam us down`, then `Asuiar, who are you`.
6. **qQkq**, at 7500.12000, the same way: `qQkq, who are you`.
7. **The Thshhath**, at 6560.12500, the same way: `Thshhath, who are you`. It says to "use the tunnel at 12500.13500 to go to 6560.6000 to get the bomb! Quickly!"
8. Beaming straight there is quicker than the tunnel: up, `Renner, set coordinates at 6560.6000`, `beam us down`. You're on the dark side, beside a black time bomb, "ticking away very noisily."
9. Spock: "Captain, a low level phaser shot would defuse this bomb." Your phaser is already on stun: shoot the bomb (F6). "By defusing the bomb, the planet has been saved."

Then `Kirk to Renner`, `beam us up`, `Spock, follow me`.

## Bhrf

1. From the bridge: `Chekov, set a course for 14926.27312.25008`, `warp factor 6`. On arrival, `orbit the second planet`.
2. In the transporter room, change back into the uniform, put the phaser in your pack and take the tapes into your hands.
3. `Renner, set coordinates at 13000.3750`, `beam us down`. You're in a hologram library in the TV mall, with the Bhrf, "a tall alien creature with many green tentacles." Asked `have you seen the Klingons`, it says they went to 2567.23762.24312.
4. Walk to the historical movie library at 10000.2000, with the tapes in your hands. The Bhrfs take them as a gift, and one stays: "I will go with you in your travels and sense things for you." The rest "scurry off doing substandard Humphrey Bogart imitations."
5. `Kirk to Renner`, `beam us up`. Uhura reports "a strange little creature on the bridge!"

## Rird

### The fight

In the transporter room, put on the heatgear for later, then `Spock, follow me` and go to the bridge. Before warping in:

1. `Chekov, raise the screens`, `set phasers to full power`, and `divert power to the torpedoes` (the game's "full torpedo power").
2. `set a course for 2567.23762.24312`, `warp factor 6`.

Two ships come at you in the 7044 Apodis system: a "Kaza-class destroyer", which is really a Rird ship in disguise, and the Klingon battlecruiser Menekku, the real enemy. The Federation never shoots first.

3. When the Menekku appears, `Chekov, lock photon torpedoes on Menekku` (the word "photon" is needed).
4. When it fires on you, `fire torpedoes`. One hit, and the fight is over: the disguised ship shows itself, and Chekov reports "Rird ship departing, sir."

Firing late, or not at all, gets the screens overloaded and the ship badly damaged; a damaged transporter means you can't land on Rird.

### The crashed ship

1. `Chekov, orbit the third planet`, then go to the transporter room. `Kirk to Chekov`, `lower the screens`, then `Renner, set coordinates at 7000.7300`, `beam us down`.
2. You're at a crash site in a hot desert, with the Rirder and their burning ship at 7105.7410. Don't walk toward the ship: it kills you.
3. `Kirk to Renner`, `set coordinates at 7105.7410`, `beam up the Rirder`. The crew of the burning ship appear in the transporter room, and in thanks the Rird captain gives you a stealth device, "something more valuable: hardware and documentation for a stealth device of our make."

### The device

1. Put the phaser in your pack and take the device (F4). Walk to Engineering.
2. `Kirk to Scotty`, `come to me`, and wait for him to arrive ("Scott enters").
3. `Scotty, analyze the device`, then `results`. Analyzing it installs it: "I've plugged her into the warp generator with a patch through the main screens."
4. Take the magnetic bottle of antimatter from Engineering, carry it to the transporter room, and drop it there (F9) for Renner.

## The protostar

1. From the bridge: `Chekov, set a course for 8572.27041.27324`, `warp factor 6`. The warp drops out at a Federation message buoy: "the standard Federation code MM-711. It may be a message from Sulu."
2. Now turn the cloak on: `Kirk to Scotty`, `turn the device on`. Scotty must be in Engineering to do it. The cloak lasts about 3 3/4 game minutes before it burns out, so don't turn it on any earlier.
3. `Chekov, warp factor 6`. In the Coalsack Normae, several Klingon vessels "soar quietly past you, out into open space": the cloak fools them. There's a huge metal framework of machine installations here, an immense glowing star, and Klingon warships.
4. Spock: "Utterly fascinating... I recommend we orbit the star. I sense a presence here trying to communicate, Captain." `Spock, mindmeld`: "... captain, we need ... orbit the star ... great energy ... bring it to life..."
5. `Chekov, orbit the star`. About 20 seconds after you're in orbit, the Klingons' pressor beams crush the Enterprise, cloak or no cloak, so be quick.
6. `Kirk to Renner`, `set coordinates at 5000.5000`, `beam down the antimatter`.

Done this way, the antimatter goes down, but the star goes nova: "you have failed your mission, failed Starfleet, and failed the quadrillions of innocent lives depending on you to keep a terrible secret from being released in the Universe as a weapon." The test evaluation is 577; the game's high score is 2001.

The game has a good ending too, in which "a star is being born. So is something else", and Spock, in the mindmeld, falls back in his seat with a cry. What it takes is still to be found. This route skips most of the game, and the good ending may depend on something it leaves out: the message buoy (Uhura can analyze messages), the Heinlein at Ascella (3060.28172.25879), or the clues on the other planets.
