## Stardate

Stardate is © 2026 Jeff Nyman. It is free software, licensed under the GNU General Public
License, version 3 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).

Stardate is a DOS PC emulator for Simon & Schuster's *Star Trek* interactive fiction, made to
preserve the original games as they were. It plays *Star Trek: The Kobayashi Alternative*,
*Star Trek: The Promethean Prophecy* and *Star Trek: First Contact*, which are included in this
repository.

Stardate's emulator core began as a copy of [Confederation](https://github.com/jeffnyman/confederation)'s,
by the same author and under the same license. Parts of Confederation's core were derived from
DOSBox, © the DOSBox Team, under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later; so is the
PC's ROM font Stardate draws its text with (`src/Stardate.Core/Fonts`), taken from DOSBox
Staging's `int10_memory.cpp`.
